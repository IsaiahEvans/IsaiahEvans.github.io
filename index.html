01 About Me

Charlotte, NC

I'm a student at Central Piedmont Community College working toward an Associate in Arts along with a stack of IT certificates, with plans to transfer into the Computer Science program at UNC Charlotte.

Outside of class I run Streamline Studios, an automotive photography and videography business here in the Charlotte area. Shooting cars taught me that the technical side and the creative side aren't actually separate — framing a shot and structuring a page come from the same instinct for making something people want to look at.

I'm taking WEB140 because front-end development sits right where those two interests meet. I've already earned CPCC certificates in Front-End Development Fundamentals, IT-JavaScript, IT-Java, and IT Fundamentals, and this course is where I get to put them together into something actually published. By the end of the semester I want this page to be a portfolio I'd be comfortable sending to an employer.
